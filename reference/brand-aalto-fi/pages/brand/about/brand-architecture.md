## Brand architecture

<!-- layout: theme=red -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Brands are built to unify around a purpose and create a meaningful relationship with our stakeholders.

The value propositions of brand types in the architecture should support the objectives of and contribute to the brand equity of the master brand.

When building Aalto University brand types, we should focus on entities that have the potential to become meaningful hero entities of the university brand.

To do this, we need clearly defined requirements and criteria.

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

###### Key expressions in our model

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: content_tile_collection -->

<!-- layout: tiles_per_row=3, theme=neutral, skim=none -->
###### "Master brand"

A master brand is an overarching brand name that serves as the main anchoring point on which all underlying entities and sub-brands are based.

<!-- layout: image_aspect_ratio=hd -->
###### “Brand architecture”

A description of a master brand -led portfolio including different brand types, their role in the portfolio and relationship to each other (including the master brand, e.g. endorsement).

<!-- layout: image_aspect_ratio=hd -->
###### “Brand type”

A subset of the architecture model, an umbrella for similar brands within the portfolio defining their criteria, common brand assets and elements.

<!-- layout: image_aspect_ratio=hd -->

<!-- slice: highlight_section -->

What makes it a brand?

**1.** Brand has an identified target group.
**2.** Brand has a value proposition which is relevant to its stakeholders and supports the objectives of the master brand.
**3.** Brand has set targets, what it aims to achieve.
**4.** Brand has the potential to operate across time, platforms, touchpoints, and formats.
**5.** Brand has a defined and identifiable identity.
**6.** Brand should have a budget.
**7.** Brand should have an owner with long-term commitment.
**8.** Brand should be legally protected.

### What makes a brand?

<!-- layout: alignment=right -->
##### 1. Target group

Brand has an identified target audience that it serves.

##### 2. Value proposition

Brand has a value proposition which is relevant to its stakeholders and supports the objectives of the master brand.

##### 3. Target

Brand has set targets, what it aims to achieve.

##### 4. Agnostic

Brand has the potential to operate across time, platforms, touchpoints, and formats.

##### 5. Identity

Brand has a defined and identifiable identity.

##### 6. Budget

Brand should have a budget.

##### 7. Owner

Brand should have an owner with long-term commitment.

##### 8. Legal protection

Brand should be legally protected.

###### Brand types

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: text_and_image_section -->

**Master**
Aalto University & Aalto Uni -wide entities such as services, Virtual Campus Tour, ACRIS, Networking Platform, Research, Impact, Cross-cutting themes, Lifewide Learning

**Core (e.g. schools)**
Core of Aalto University operations relating to research, education, impact.

**Focus**
Highlight uniqueness and key brand differentiators of Aalto University (e.g. Marsio).

**Flexible**
Student unions & associations (AYY, KY, TOKYO etc.), student hobby clubs, Aaltoes, A Blanc, A Bloc

**Non-brands**
These use master or core brand assets: departments, research projects, research groups, services, platforms, infrastructure, impact, EDI, cross-cutting themes

**accordion_section[0].title:** 1. Is it owned by Aalto University?

If no: Check if flexible is applicable, if not abandon.

If yes: see next question.

**accordion_section[1].title:** 2. Is it either core operations or differentiating us?

If no: Use brand assets of MASTER / CORE / FOCUS.

If yes: see next question (3)

**accordion_section[2].title:** 3. Is it a singular project?

If no:  see next question (4)

If yes: Use brand assets of MASTER / CORE / FOCUS.

If yes but meaningful: [Contact](/support) Brand and Creative team to further elaborate why it needs its own identity. E.g. Arts 150 years.

**accordion_section[3].title:** 4. Does it have a potential to become a brand?

If no: Use brand assets of MASTER / CORE / FOCUS.

If yes: [Contact](/support) Brand and Creative team to further elaborate why it should become a brand instead of utilising existing brands / assets.
