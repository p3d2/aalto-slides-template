###### Data visualisation

Another meaningful area is data visualisation (information graphics), where a higher level of sophistication can be reached when cooperating with a visual designer. Data visualisation ranges from the design of traditional statistical graphs to interactive and creative solutions for browsing highly complex data sets.

However, it is recommended that only colours and typefaces belonging to the visual identity of Aalto University are used and that the work is marked or deputized by an appropriate logo to give context.

###### Illustrations

A picture conveys more than a thousand words. Illustrations bring warmth and fresh narrative aspects to your message. The style and feel of illustrations should always defer to subject matter. However, generally it is best to strive for a manufactured feeling as opposed to a crafted, hand-drawn look.
