## Images

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

With our photography, we want to celebrate the diversity of people, activities and creativity that thrive on our campus. We want to depict collaboration and teamwork.

Our aim is to create high-quality images that feel authentic and invoke curiosity. We want to create stunning compositions that convey energy and beauty. We want to avoid images that feel overly posed or heavily retouched.

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

The use of **stock imagery** should be avoided.

In rare cases, stock images can be used to illustrate the larger context to which the work at Aalto is related. For example, if we need a photograph of a desert to illustrate research related to climate change, we could use stock footage. However, unique pictures of the solutions developed at Aalto can often be more effective than generic stock pictures.

In some cases, like in quantum physics where things cannot be seen, metaphorical images or illustrations might be needed instead of photographs.

**AI-generated images** should be avoided when the photograph is intended to be viewed as a representation of real life. As a general rule, we want to be authentic in Aalto University communications when we portray our people, our campus, and infrastructure. However, there are other contexts, such as research, education, and arts projects where AI generated images are and can be used.

In most cases, we do not recommend publishing AI-generated images on [aalto.fi](http://aalto.fi/) due to copyright and brand alignment issues.

###### Note

Prior permission is required from individuals who will be featured prominently in the photographs. Read more about the [right of use and copyright of photographs, videos and audio files on aalto.fi

Photographed individuals should rather not wear large logos or slogans on their clothing,  except for official Aalto University branding.

Aalto University has a constantly evolving material bank. These images can be used for internal and external communications. When using photos, make sure you credit the images to the university and the photographer like so: Aalto University / FirstName LastName
