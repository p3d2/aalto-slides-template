This section contains Aalto’s logo system. It consists of core components, the logomark ("A") versions, and the wordmark (Aalto University). Aalto logos are available in three languages: Finnish, Swedish, and English.

The three "A" logomark versions are meant to be used randomly. However, sometimes they can be used purposefully in order to amplify the message at hand. For example, if you are promoting an event where talks are the key content, it’s advisable to use the "A” logomark. Or if your communication material is posing a question, it’s natural to use version "A?". And when you want to underline the gravity of the message, the "A!" version is the obvious choice.

Get to know the logo system below or go to Logo generator to download correct logo for your use.

Aalto University’s tertiary logos are intended for various contexts where space is limited, such as campus signage, A4 document letterheads, or diplomas. Tertiary logos should be used sparingly and deliberately where purposeful. ###### Use of black and white logos

The university logos exist in two colours: black and white. The old mixed-colour versions of the logo are no longer official Aalto University logos and should never be used.

The black logo versions should be used with white or light colour backgrounds or images – whereas the white logo versions should be used on black or dark colour backgrounds or images.

The aim is to always ensure maximum contrast between the logo and the background. To determine whether black or white should be used, check the darkness of the background on which the logo is placed. If an image is used as background to the logo, it should be sufficiently calm and even in tone for the logo to be clearly visible. For more information on how to use the logo with our brand colours, please see the colour accessibility definitions section.
The same colour principles mentioned here apply to all logos.
