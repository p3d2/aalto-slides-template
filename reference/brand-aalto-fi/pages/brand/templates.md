<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

###### Browse templates and presentations

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

A design template is a file with an overall layout and format to be used for a group of documents. With a template, the layout has already been designed with placeholder text and images to be replaced with information relevant to you. A design template defines the colour scheme, font style, and format. When using templates, please pay attention to the grid, alignment, and consistency of document language with logo language.

**Note** that most templates are only downloadable by Aalto personnel from the material bank. All Aalto personnel should **log into the material bank with HAKA login.
