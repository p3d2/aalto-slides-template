## Tone of voice

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: highlight_section -->

It’s not just what we say. It’s how we say it.

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: content_tile_collection -->

<!-- layout: tiles_per_row=3, theme=neutral, skim=none -->
###### We are engaging in order to get people involved

An engaging brand is authentic, meaningful and brings value to the world. These attributes make us relevant and trustworthy. For example, we are honest and straightforward and aim to put all our content into a broader understandable context and inspire people to get involved.

<!-- layout: image_aspect_ratio=hd -->
###### We are courageous in order to make people feel empowered

We proudly share our successes and are ready to offer solutions to major societal challenges. We are not afraid of taking a stand. We say what we think but never in an arrogant way. For example, we use fewer qualifiers like “possibly” and “maybe” and avoid ambiguity.

<!-- layout: image_aspect_ratio=hd -->
###### We are trustworthy in order to make people well informed

Honesty, responsibility, and reliability should be seen in our communication. We articulate our intentions clearly and back them up with fact-based, up-to-date, and transparent content. Washing in any colour, be it green, blue, pink, or white, is a no-go. This way we fulfill the audience’s need to know.
