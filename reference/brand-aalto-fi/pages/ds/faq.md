## Frequently asked questions

Whether you're a designer, developer, or another stakeholder, we understand that you may have inquiries about our Aalto Design System and how it can benefit your projects. While our FAQ section is a work in progress, we plan to add more questions and answers soon. If you have a specific inquiry not covered here, please contact our Design System team for assistance.

**accordion_section[0].title:** What is the Design System all about?

The Design System is the documentation of all the digital resources, elements, and instructions, that make up Aalto University’s digital sites and services. It serves as a framework that helps maintain a consistent user experience throughout all digital services and touchpoints, ultimately contributing both to the university's brand identity and user satisfaction.

**accordion_section[1].title:** What's inside the Design System?

The Design System consists of reusable design assets, component libraries, and guidelines. A shared set of design guidelines and elements enables the teams to work more efficiently, facilitating collaboration among different teams and departments within the university. This leads to faster and more consistent design iterations and content development and helps avoid user experience fragmentation.

**accordion_section[2].title:** What if I can't find a component, module or section that I need?

The Design System is a collaborative effort, and we continuously design and develop components based on our community's needs. We encourage you to [contact the Design system team](/support), and let us know more about your need.

**accordion_section[3].title:** Frameworks we use are not supported by the design system. What can we do?

We are continuously developing and broadening the design system to better support the entire Aalto community. We will be updating the [roadmap](https://brand.aalto.fi/ds/about/releases), check if we're already planning on broadening the design system to support your needs. If not, please drop us a line so we can discuss further!

We are also developing a contribution model so that the entire community can contribute their assets into the design system and share them with others.

**accordion_section[4].title:** Who can I contact for questions or support?

The Design System is maintained by the Brand and Digital Experience team in Aalto University Communications. We value all questions and comments in developing the Design System better. We're also here for your support, please, don't hesitate to contact us!
