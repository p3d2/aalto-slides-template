###### Overview

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

While the high-level UI elements offered by the design system are designed to be versatile, there may be scenarios where they don't fully align with your requirements. In such cases, you have the flexibility to leverage only specific parts of the design system to construct your own unique UI elements, or even explore new technologies that better suit your needs.

It's worth noting that such customisations may be valuable to other teams as well. Don't hesitate to [reach out to the design system team](/support) to discuss your specific requirements and best approach. It's possible that alternative solutions are already on the roadmap, or your contribution might even be integrated directly into the core library, benefiting the wider user community.
