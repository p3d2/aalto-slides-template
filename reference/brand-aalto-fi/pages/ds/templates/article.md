## Article

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Article template is a content-rich layout for publishing news, research, or editorial pieces. Typically includes a prominent title, author info, featured image, and structured body text with optional subheadings, quotes, and related contacts or links.
