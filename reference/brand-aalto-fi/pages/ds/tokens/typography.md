## Typography

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

The typography tokens provide a complete set of definitions for all typographic elements, including fonts, font styles, line heights, and more. These tokens should be comprehensive enough for designing and implementing all textual content on any digital Aalto channel.

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->
