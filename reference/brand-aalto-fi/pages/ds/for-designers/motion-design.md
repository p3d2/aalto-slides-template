## Motion design

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

The role of motion in Aalto Design System is to help and guide the user. The aim is to make interactions easy to understand and create spatial awareness. Motion should also create a feeling of functional and polished interfaces.

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

###### Motion principles

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Motion principles are the cornerstones of which every motion interaction should be based on.

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: content_tile_collection -->

<!-- layout: tiles_per_row=4, theme=neutral, skim=top -->
###### Functional

Motion is used to help the user understand what will happen, what is happening and what has happened.

<!-- layout: image_aspect_ratio=hd -->
###### Authentic

Motion is inherently digital and consequential from interaction to interaction.

<!-- layout: image_aspect_ratio=hd -->
###### Accessible

Never flickering, never overwhelming and always with the option to be reduced.

<!-- layout: image_aspect_ratio=hd -->
###### Inspiring

Motion is used to highlight, emphasise and promote content.

<!-- layout: image_aspect_ratio=hd -->

<!-- slice: content_tile_collection -->

<!-- layout: tiles_per_row=1, theme=neutral, skim=none -->
###### Reduced motion

All services designed with the Aalto design system should have the ability to correspond to operation system wide reduced motion settings. People with motion sensitivities should only encounter motion that is absolutely essential to understanding the interface, all other motion should be disabled.

###### Motion in use

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Motion is used sparingly in web interfaces, while in mobile applications more motion can be added. There should always be an option to use reduced motion.

The main use cases for motion are transitions and micro interactions. It can also be used to highlight content.

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

###### Transitions

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Transitions are changes from one screen or page to another. In these cases motion is used to guide the user and help create spatial awareness inside an application.

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: content_tile_collection -->

<!-- layout: tiles_per_row=3, theme=neutral, skim=top -->
