## Anchor Links

Anchor Links take the user to another location on the same page.

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Anchor Links provide a convenient way to navigate within a lengthy webpage or document without the need to scroll manually. When the user clicks on an Anchor Link, the page automatically scrolls to the corresponding anchor point, bringing the desired content into view.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Anchor Links are most useful with long-form articles, where swiftly accessing specific sections is beneficial and would require an extensive amount of scrolling. In addition to their linking functionality, Anchor Links serve as a helpful table of contents, contributing to improved content comprehension by offering a visual representation of a page's structure and organisation.

The horizontal variant is the preferred one. However, the vertical layout is used if the anchor labels are longer than a couple of words.

In the majority of cases, it is advisable for Anchor Links to remain fixed at the top of a page, even when scrolling beyond their initial position. There are instances where this sticky behavior may not be feasible though, such as when the viewport size is limited.
