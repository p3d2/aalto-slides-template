## Title

Title describes what the content is about.

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Titles give a brief summary of the content and have a larger font size than the body text. They are easy to read on screens.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Titles point out the important things and what each text section is about. They create hierarchy and rhythm on the page and make it scannable for the reader. Titles should be brief and concise. Use Titles when there is a new theme or section in the text. Do not use Titles alone without other content.
