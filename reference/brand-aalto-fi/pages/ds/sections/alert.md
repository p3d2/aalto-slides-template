## Alert

Alert shows urgent information which needs to capture the user's attention.

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Alerts inform about changes or conditions that are upcoming or ongoing. Alerts appear at the top of the screen, and users can dismiss them.
