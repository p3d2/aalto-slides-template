## Hero

Hero immediately engages the users.

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Hero area is at the top of the page. Its purpose is to engage users with interesting and descriptive images and texts. Hero gives information about the topic on the page, and it creates hierarchy and rhythm in the layout.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

The Hero component serves as a prominent and engaging element that effectively communicates the "what, where, and why" of a page. It captivates the user's attention and guides them toward the next essential step in their journey.

It's important to employ the various Hero variants appropriately throughout your website. The primary Hero variant should be reserved exclusively for the front page (root page) of the site. This ensures that the most impactful and crucial messaging is delivered to users right from the start. By utilising different levels of Heros throughout the site, you can effectively convey the navigational hierarchy, allowing users to gain a clear understanding of the site structure and easily navigate through its content.
