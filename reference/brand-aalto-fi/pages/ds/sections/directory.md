## Directory

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

The Directory section displays a structured list of related items, such as people, sessions or works, in a compact format. Each entry includes a prominent label with optional supporting details, arranged for easy scanning and comparison. An optional ingress text can be included to introduce the content and provide context.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Use the Directory to present grouped content that shares a common structure or context. It is suitable for programmes, collections or overviews where clarity and consistency are essential. Depending on the content type and level of detail, a more visual section like a [Card Collection](/ds/sections/card-collection) or a [Content Box Collection](/ds/sections/content-box-collection) may sometimes be a better fit.
