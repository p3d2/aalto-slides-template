## Card Strip

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Card Strip displays multiple [Cards](/ds/modules/card) in a horizontally scrollable container, allowing users to browse content efficiently.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Use Card Strip when presenting multiple [Cards](/ds/modules/card) with dynamic content, where users can explore them at their own pace without needing to view every item. If all Cards must be seen, consider using a [Card Collection](/ds/sections/card-collection) instead. Depending on the content type, alternatives like [Content Box Strip](/ds/sections/content-box-strip) or [Image Tile Strip](/ds/sections/image-tile-strip) might be more suitable.
