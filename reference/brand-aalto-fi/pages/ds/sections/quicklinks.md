## Quick Links

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Quick Links offers a means to emphasise a set of crucial links on a page, each featuring link text and an icon.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

The Quick Links section generally accommodates two or three links, and it is advisable to limit it to one section per page. Depending on link importance, you may also explore other sections like [CTA](/ds/sections/cta) for a single link or [Liftup Collection](/ds/sections/liftup-collection) / [Card Collection](/ds/sections/card-collection) for a more extensive set of links.
