## Date Picker Modal

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Date Picker Modal encapsulates a [Date Picker](/ds/modules/date-picker) inside a full-screen [Modal](/ds/sections/modal), providing users with a focused, immersive interface for selecting dates.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

The Date Picker Modal is useful in scenarios where date selection is a critical part of the process and the user needs a distraction-free environment to make a choice. It is commonly used in mobile designs where a full-screen modal provides more space for date selection, or when selecting dates is a primary task within a workflow, such as booking premises or setting up events. See [Date Picker](/ds/modules/date-picker) and [Date Input](/ds/modules/date-input) for alternative solutions.
