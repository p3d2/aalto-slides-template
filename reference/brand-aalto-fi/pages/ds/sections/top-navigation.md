## Top Navigation

Top Navigation is a horizontal navigation located at the top of the screen.

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Top Navigation is the primary means for users to navigate within a site or service. It provides an easy way to reach the main destinations of the site.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Top Navigation is a mandatory element for practically all Aalto services and sites, and all the pages within them. The navigation is always positioned at the top of the page and has a specified order for actions such as search, language selection, and profile.
