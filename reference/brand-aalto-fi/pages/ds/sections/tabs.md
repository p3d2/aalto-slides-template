###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Tabs require at least two parallel views. They are not a primary way of navigating, and shouldn't be used when there are more than eight parallel views. Tabs enable easy navigation between related content, but they are not used to indicate progress or to compare the information.
