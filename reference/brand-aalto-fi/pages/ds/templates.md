## Templates

Templates are complete page-level layouts that provide a consistent structure for common content types and use cases across channels. They combine predefined sections into coherent compositions, helping teams quickly assemble visually consistent pages that are fast to implement.

###### Using the templates

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Unlike components, modules, and sections, templates are not implemented as UI components in Storybook. This is because they are flexible by nature, intended to be adapted based on content, and can be easily assembled by placing sections in the desired order. For design reference, all templates are available in the Figma Starter Kit.
