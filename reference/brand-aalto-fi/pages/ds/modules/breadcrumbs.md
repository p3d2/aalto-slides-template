## Breadcrumbs

Breadcrumbs show the navigation path to the current page.

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Breadcrumbs aid users to keep track of their location within digital services. Breadcrumbs create awareness of information hierarchy and help navigate back to previous locations.
