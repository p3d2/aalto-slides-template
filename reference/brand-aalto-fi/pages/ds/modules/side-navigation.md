## Side navigation

Side navigation is a vertical navigation typically located on the left side of the screen.

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Side Navigation provides quick access to key sections of a site or application. It is a vertical, hierarchical element typically placed on the left side of the screen. It includes a [Navigation accordion](/ds/modules/navigation-accordion) module for expandable content, and may also feature action links or a sticky footer for persistent controls.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Side navigation is typically positioned on the left and top side of the screen, providing convenient access to important content. It is best suited for desktop layouts where vertical space supports clear hierarchy. On mobile devices, the screen size may not accommodate a Side navigation at all, but alternative navigation options should be implemented using for example [Top navigation](/ds/sections/top-navigation) or in-page links instead.
