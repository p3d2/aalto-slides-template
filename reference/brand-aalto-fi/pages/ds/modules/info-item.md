## Info item

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Info Item displays a concise piece of information, usually consisting of a title and body text. It may also include a badge, tags, or a supplementary link when relevant.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Use Info Item to present contextual or supporting details in a compact format. It is often used in groups within modules such as [Info](/ds/modules/info), [Directory Block](/ds/modules/directory-block), or [Hero](/ds/sections/hero). A single Info Item may also appear alongside articles, blog posts, or other long-form content for example to show the author, publication date, or estimated reading time.
