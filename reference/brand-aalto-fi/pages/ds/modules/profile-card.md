## Profile Card

Profile Card presents the user’s information.

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Profile Card presents the user’s information in one place. It includes name, title, and contact information. It displays the user’s avatar and shows additional info if any is available.
