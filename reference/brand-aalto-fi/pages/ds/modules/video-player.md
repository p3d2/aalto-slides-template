## Video Player

The Video Player is a component designed for showcasing video content.

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Video Player is an interactive component featuring a video element and essential playback controls. Depending on the specific context and requirements, you can select from a couple of different variants to suit your needs.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

When incorporating decorative videos, like those used as element backgrounds, it's best to opt for the simplest player variant. This minimalist player autoplays the video and includes only the most essential user interface element: pause / play button. In adherence to accessibility guidelines, it is imperative to always include a pause option within a video player.

However, for longer videos that resemble movies or require more user interaction, it's more suitable to select the player with full playhead controls. This comprehensive player allows users to have more control over their video-watching experience, including the ability to seek to a specific moment in the video.
