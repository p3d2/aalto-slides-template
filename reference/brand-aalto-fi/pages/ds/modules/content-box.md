## Content Box

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Content Box presents short, focused pieces of text. It is non-interactive and commonly displays a single statement or a paragraph that supports the surrounding content.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Use a Content Box to convey the key message of a page, such as values, themes, or essential takeaways. It works well in structured layouts where clarity and rhythm are essential, typically presented in groups using a [Content Box Collection](/ds/sections/content-box-collection) or a [Content Box Strip](/ds/sections/content-box-strip). To preserve impact and avoid visual noise, it is best to include only a few Content Boxes in a single collection.

If the content needs to lead to another page, use [Card](/ds/modules/card). If it requires an action such as downloading, use [Liftup](/ds/modules/liftup). For purely visual content, use [Image Tile](/ds/modules/image-tile).
