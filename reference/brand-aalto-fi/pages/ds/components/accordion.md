## Accordion

Accordion allows user to toggle between hiding and showing content.

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Accordion consists of items, which have clickable headers and corresponding content sections. Users can open and close sections as needed, and this allows them to view and interact with the content efficiently.
