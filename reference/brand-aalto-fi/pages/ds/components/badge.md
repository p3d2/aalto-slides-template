## Badge

Badges are static visual labels.

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Badges are visual text labels that indicate status. Badges are not interactive, so a user can't add or remove them.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Badges label elements. They can communicate status, show number counts, or highlight specific features. Badges include only static numerical or textual information and are not active. When interactive labels are needed, use [Tags](/ds/components/tag) or [Chips](/ds/components/chip) instead.
