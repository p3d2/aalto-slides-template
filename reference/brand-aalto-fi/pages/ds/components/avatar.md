## Avatar

Avatar is a visual representation of a user.

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Avatar displays a profile photo, initials, or a fallback profile icon. The avatar should be linked to the user's profile view or profile page.

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

###### Examples

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

###### With profile photo

Avatar can display a photo of the person. This is the preferred variant.

**page_section_element.component_id:** components/avatar--image

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

###### With initials

If a photo is not available, Avatar can also display the initials of the person.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Avatars are usually used with login credentials to represent the user, provide a personal touch, and visually identify users.
