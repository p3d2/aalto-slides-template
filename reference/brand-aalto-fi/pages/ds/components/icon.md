###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Icons are typically used inside other user interface elements to serve as visual cues to convey information quickly and intuitively. They enhance the usability of the interface by providing clear and concise representations of e.g. actions, functions, or content categories.
