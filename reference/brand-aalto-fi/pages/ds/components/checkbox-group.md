###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

As a group, checkboxes are used for allowing multiple choices in forms, tables, lists, and filters. Checkboxes are not used when only one option can be selected. An exception to this is checkboxes that are used to give consent and confirm choices, for example when agreeing to terms and conditions. They are not typically used for on/off settings, aside from confirming choices - consider [Switch](/ds/components/switch) for such cases. When only one option can be selected, you should use a [Radio Group](/ds/components/radio-group) instead.
