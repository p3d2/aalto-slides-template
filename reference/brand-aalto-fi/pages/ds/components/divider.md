## Divider

Divider splits a page or an element into sections.

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Dividers are subtle lines that separate content on a page. They can be placed either horizontally or vertically between elements.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Divider is used to divide content for example within cards, tables, lists, or menus. It is generally used when white space is not enough. 

Divider is not used to create borders or underline content. It is also not used between card components.
