## Progress indicator

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Progress indicator displays an indefinite loading spinner for indicating an ongoing process. It provides visual feedback to users, indicating that the system is processing their request or performing a task.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Use the Progress Indicator to inform users about processes that do not have a specific duration or completion time, such as fetching data from a server or performing background tasks. If the process duration is known, consider using the [Progress Bar](/ds/components/progress-bar) component instead to provide more accurate progress indication. If the loading process is expected to take a long time, consider providing additional context or feedback to users to manage their expectations.
