## Checkbox

Checkboxes allow binary choices from predefined options.

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Checkboxes are a selection of controls that enable users to select one or more options from a set.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Checkboxes typically form [Checkbox groups](/ds/components/checkbox-group). An exception to this is checkboxes that are used to give consent and confirm choices, for example when agreeing to terms and conditions.

Checkboxes should not be used when only one of the many options can be selected. In such cases, a [radio group](/ds/components/radio-group) is usually a more fitting choice.

For toggling binary options on/off, you may also consider using a [Switch](/ds/components/switch).
