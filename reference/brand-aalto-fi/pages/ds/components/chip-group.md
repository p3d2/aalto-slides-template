## Chip group

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Chip Group is a wrapper for arranging multiple [Chips](/ds/components/chip) together in a single layout. It manages spacing and alignment across the set.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Use Chip Group to display a collection of Chips that represent active filters, selected items, or similar elements. It ensures visual consistency when Chips are used in groups. Keep the set manageable in size to support readability and ease of interaction.
