## Progress Bar

<!-- layout: theme=black -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

A progress bar component provides dynamic visual feedback on the completion status of a task or process. Whether it's a straightforward loading indication or a complex operation divided into stages, this component offers clarity and transparency in tracking progress of a long-lasting operation.

###### Usage

<!-- layout: alignment=left, theme=neutral -->

<!-- slice: page_section_element -->

Progress bar is suitable for a wide range of applications, from simple file uploads to more intricate multi-step processes. Depending on the specific use case, the Progress bar allows for animated indicators or precise progress representation. If the duration of the process is unknown and it is not possible to display a percentage completed, it is advisable to use [Progress Indicator](/ds/components/progress-indicator) component instead.
